## 1. Qisqacha ta'rif
Hosila ($f'(x)$ yoki $\frac{df}{dx}$) — funksiya argumenti $x$ o'zgarganda funksiya qiymati $f(x)$ ning o'zgarish tezligini ifodalovchi matematik analiz tushunchasi.

## 2. Nima uchun kerak?
Neyron tarmoqlarda [Backpropagation](/learn/backpropagation) orqali xatolik signalini orqaga uzatishda har bir qatlamning aktivatsiya funksiyasi differensiallanuvchi bo'lishi shart. Aktivatsiya funksiyasining birinchi tartibli hosilasi $f'(\text{net}_i)$ xatolik signalini qanchalik o'tkazish yoki so'ndirishini belgilaydi.

## 3. Matematik Asoslari: Sigmoid Hosilasi va Saturation (Level 1 — Level 3)
- **Logistik Sigmoid Funksiyasi va Uning Maksimal Hosilasi (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, 1997, Section 3):**
  $$ \sigma(x) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-x}}, \quad \sigma'(x) = \sigma(x)\left(1 - \sigma(x)\right) $$
  Logistik sigmoid hosilasining maksimal qiymati $x = 0$ nuqtada kuzatiladi va u $0.25$ ga teng:
  $$ \max_x \sigma'(x) = 0.5 \times (1 - 0.5) = 0.25 $$
  Agar kirish qiymati $|x|$ juda kattalashib ketsa, funksiya to'yinish (saturation) hududiga tushib qoladi va $\sigma'(x) \to 0$ bo'lib, gradient oqimi to'xtab qoladi (Rumelhart et al., 1986; Ioffe & Szegedy, 2015; Vaswani et al., 2017).

- **Chiziqli Ayniyat Funksiyasi (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, 1997):**
  $$ f_j(x) = x \implies f'_j(x) = 1.0 $$
  [LSTM](/learn/lstm) ning Constant Error Carousel (CEC) markazida hosila doimo 1.0 bo'lishi uchun aynan shu funksiya ishlatiladi.

### Qadam-baqadam Sonli Misol
$\sigma(x) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-x}}$ funksiyaning $x = 0$ va $x = 2$ nuqtalardagi hosilasini hisoblaymiz:

1. **$x = 0$ da:**
   $$ \sigma(0) = \frac{1}{1 + 1} = 0.5 \implies \sigma'(0) = 0.5 \times (1 - 0.5) = 0.25 $$

2. **$x = 2$ da:**
   $$ \sigma(2) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-2}} \approx \frac{1}{1 + 0.1353} \approx 0.8808 $$
   $$ \sigma'(2) = 0.8808 \times (1 - 0.8808) \approx 0.1050 $$
