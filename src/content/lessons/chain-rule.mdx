## 1. Qisqacha ta'rif
Chain Rule (Zanjir qoidasi) — murakkab kompozitsion funksiyalar hosilasini ichki va tashqi hosilalar ko'paytmasi (tarmoqlangan hisoblash graflarida esa barcha keyingi iste'molchi yo'llar yig'indisi) sifatida hisoblash qoidasi.

## 2. Nima uchun kerak?
Chuqur neyron tarmoqlarda [Backpropagation](/learn/backpropagation) (Rumelhart et al., 1986), vaqt bo'yicha xatolik oqimi (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, 1997) va [Batch Normalization](/learn/batch-normalization) orqali gradient o'tishi (Ioffe & Szegedy, 2015) to'liq Chain Rule ga asoslanadi.

## 3. Matematik Formulalari (Level 1 — Level 3)
- **Tarmoqlangan Grafda Yig'indi (Rumelhart et al., 1986):**
  Agar yashirin birlik $a_j$ keyingi qatlamdagi bir nechta $z_k$ birliklariga ulansa, teskari o'tishda barcha $k$ yo'llar bo'yicha yig'indi olinadi:
  $$ \frac{\partial E}{\partial a_j} = \sum_{k} \frac{\partial E}{\partial z_k} \cdot \frac{\partial z_k}{\partial a_j} $$

- **Ketma-ket Qadamlar Bo'yicha Ko'paytma (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, 1997):**
  $q$ ta vaqt qadami bo'yicha orqaga uzatilgan xatolik har bir qadamdagi hosila va vazn ko'paytmasini o'z ichiga oladi:
  $$ \prod_{m=1}^{q} f'_{l_m} w_{l_m l_{m-1}} $$

### Qadam-baqadam Sonli Misol (Ikki Chiqishli Tarmoqda Chain Rule)
Faraz qilaylik, yashirin birlik aktivatsiyasi $a_h = 2.0$ ikkita chiqishga ulanadi:
- 1-chiqish: $z_1 = w_1 a_h$ ($w_1 = 3.0$) va uning xatolik hosilasi $\frac{\partial E}{\partial z_1} = 0.5$.
- 2-chiqish: $z_2 = w_2 a_h$ ($w_2 = -1.0$) va uning xatolik hosilasi $\frac{\partial E}{\partial z_2} = 0.8$.

Yashirin birlik $a_h$ bo'yicha umumiy hosilani topamiz:
1. 1-chiqishdan kelgan ulush:
   $$ \frac{\partial E}{\partial z_1} \cdot \frac{\partial z_1}{\partial a_h} = 0.5 \times 3.0 = 1.5 $$
2. 2-chiqishdan kelgan ulush:
   $$ \frac{\partial E}{\partial z_2} \cdot \frac{\partial z_2}{\partial a_h} = 0.8 \times (-1.0) = -0.8 $$
3. Ikkala yo'l yig'indisi:
   $$ \frac{\partial E}{\partial a_h} = 1.5 + (-0.8) = 0.7 $$
