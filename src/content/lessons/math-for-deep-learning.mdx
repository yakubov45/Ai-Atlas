## 1. Qisqacha ta'rif
Math for Deep Learning — neyron tarmoqlarda ma'lumotlarni vektorlashgan tenzorlar sifatida tasvirlash, oldinga hisoblash (forward computation), teskari differensiallash ([Backpropagation](/learn/backpropagation)) va stoxastik optimallashtirishning nazariy asosini tashkil etuvchi to'rtta matematik yo'nalish: Linear Algebra, Calculus, Probability and Statistics hamda Optimization majmuasidir.

## 2. To'rtta Matematik Bo'lim va Neyron Tarmoqlar Bilan Bog'liqligi

## 1. Linear Algebra

[Vector](/learn/vector), [Matrix](/learn/matrix), [Matrix Operations](/learn/matrix-operations), [Tensor](/learn/tensor)

Kirish ma'lumotlarini ([Embeddings](/learn/embeddings), tasvirlar) va qatlam vaznlarini (W) saqlash hamda GPU da parallel ko'paytirish (Q K^T, W x + b) amallarini bajarish.

## 2. Calculus

[Hosila](/learn/hosila), [Partial Derivative](/learn/partial-derivative), [Gradient](/learn/gradient), [Chain Rule](/learn/chain-rule)

Yo'qotish funksiyasining har bir parametrga nisbatan sezgirligini hisoblash va [Backpropagation](/learn/backpropagation) orqali xatolik signalini qatlamlar bo'ylab orqaga uzatish.

## 3. Probability & Statistics

[Probability](/learn/probability), [Mean](/learn/mean), [Standard Deviation](/learn/standard-deviation)

Softmax ehtimolliklarini modellash, [Batch Normalization](/learn/batch-normalization) da aktivatsiyalarni standartlashtirish ($\mu_{\mathcal{B}}, \sigma_{\mathcal{B}}^2$), [Transformers](/learn/transformers) da $\sqrt{d_k}$ masshtablash va [Adam](/learn/adam) da 1-/2-momentlarni baholash.

## 4. Optimization

[Gradient Descent](/learn/gradient-descent), [SGD](/learn/sgd), [Adam](/learn/adam)

Yuqori o'lchamli parametrlar fazosida yo'qotish funksiyasini iterativ minimallashtirish va Decoupled Weight Decay orqali regulyarizatsiya qilish.

## 3. Matematik Nazariya va Muhandislik Implementatsiyasi Farqi
Sonli barqarorlik (Numerical Stability): Matematik tenglamalarda bo'lish amali har qanday musbat dispersiya uchun o'rinli bo'lsa, kompyuter xotirasida standart chetlanish nolga teng bo'lib qolganda normalizatsiya cheksizlikka ($\infty$) ketib qoladi. Shu sababli [Batch Normalization](/learn/batch-normalization) va [Adam](/learn/adam) algoritmlarida maxrajga kichik $\epsilon$ o'zgarmasi qo'shiladi (Ioffe & Szegedy, 2015; Kingma & Ba, 2015).
Vektorizatsiya va Mini-Batching: Matematik ta'riflarda har bir so'rov $q$ yoki namuna $x_i$ alohida yozilsa, amaliyotda ular $Q, K, V$ matritsalariga va mini-batch tenzorlariga qadoqlanib, zamonaviy GPU larda parallel hisoblanadi (Vaswani et al., 2017; Ioffe & Szegedy, 2015).
