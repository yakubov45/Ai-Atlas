## 1. Qisqacha ta'rif
Gradient ($g_t = \nabla_\theta f_t(\theta_t)$) — maqsad funksiyasi $f_t(\theta_t)$ ning barcha parametrlar bo'yicha [Partial Derivative](/learn/partial-derivative) (xususiy hosilalari) ni jamlagan vektor bo'lib, $i$-chi elementi $g_{t,i} = \frac{\partial f_t(\theta_t)}{\partial \theta_{t,i}}$ ga teng (Kingma & Ba, 2015).

## 2. Nima uchun kerak?
Gradient vektori parametrlar fazosida funksiyaning eng tez o'sish yo'nalishini ko'rsatadi. [Gradient Descent](/learn/gradient-descent), [SGD](/learn/sgd) va [Adam](/learn/adam) optimizatorlari yo'qotish funksiyasini minimallashtirish uchun aynan shu gradient vektoridan foydalanadi.

## 3. Matematik Xossalari: Masshtabga Nisbatan Invariantlik (Level 1 — Level 3)
Kingma va Ba (2015, Section 2) ko'rsatganidek:

Oddiy [SGD](/learn/sgd) da gradient vektori c koeffitsiyentga masshtablansa (c \cdot g_t), parametr yangilanishi ham c marta o'zgaradi.
[Adam](/learn/adam) da esa effektiv qadam kattaligi $\Delta_t$ gradientlarning diagonal masshtablanishiga nisbatan invariantdir: gradient $g$ ni $c$ omilga ko'paytirish 1-moment $\hat{m}_t$ ni $c$ marta, 2-moment $\hat{v}_t$ ni esa $c^2$ marta masshtablaydi va ular nisbatda qisqarib ketadi:

$$
\frac{c \cdot \hat{m}_t}{\sqrt{c^2 \cdot \hat{v}_t}} = \frac{\hat{m}_t}{\sqrt{\hat{v}_t}}
$$
Qadam-baqadam Sonli Misol
[Partial Derivative](/learn/partial-derivative) konspektidagi misolda $\frac{\partial E}{\partial w_1} = -4$ va $\frac{\partial E}{\partial w_2} = -6$ topilgan edi. Ular birgalikda 2 o'lchamli gradient vektorini tashkil etadi:

$$ \nabla_w E = \begin{bmatrix} -4 \\ -6 \end{bmatrix} $$

Agar bu gradientni $c = 10$ ga ko'paytirsak ($[-40, -60]^T$), har bir komponent uchun $\frac{c \cdot g_i}{\sqrt{c^2 \cdot g_i^2}} = \frac{g_i}{|g_i|} = [-1, -1]^T$ nisbat o'zgarmas qoladi.
