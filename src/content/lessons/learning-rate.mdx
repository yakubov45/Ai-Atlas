## 1. Qisqacha ta'rif
Learning Rate ($\alpha$, Step Size) — [Gradient Descent](/learn/gradient-descent) va stoxastik optimallashtirish algoritmlarida har bir qadamda parametrlar gradient yo'nalishi bo'yicha qanchalik katta miqdorga o'zgarishini belgilovchi giperparametr.

## 2. Nima uchun kerak?
Ioffe va Szegedy (2015) ko'rsatganidek, chuqur tarmoqlarni o'qitish odatda ehtiyotkor (kichik) o'rganish tezligini talab qiladi:

Odatda katta learning rate qatlam parametrlarining masshtabini (scale) oshirib yuboradi, bu esa [Backpropagation](/learn/backpropagation) davomida gradientlarni yanada kuchaytirib, modelning portlashiga (divergence) olib keladi. Masalan, Batch Normalization siz Inception tarmog'ida boshlang'ich learning rate ni ($\alpha = 0.0015$ dan) 5 baravar oshirish parametrlarni mashina cheksizligiga (machine infinity) olib chiqib ketgan.

Aksincha, [Batch Normalization](/learn/batch-normalization) qo'llanilganda o'qitish parametrlar masshtabiga chidamli (resilient) bo'lib, 5 baravar yuqori learning rate (BN-x5) bilan o'qitishni keskin tezlashtirish imkonini beradi.

## 3. Learning Rate Schedules va Nazariy Xossalar (Level 1 — Level 3)
Step Size Bounding va Natural Annealing (Kingma & Ba, 2015): [Adam](/learn/adam) optimizatorida qadam kattaligi $\alpha$ giperparametri bilan taxminiy chegaralangan (approximately bounded by the stepsize hyperparameter). Shuningdek, optimumga yaqinlashganda signal-shovqin nisbati ($m_t / \sqrt{v_t}$) nolga intilib, qadam kattaligining tabiiy kichrayishi (automatic step size annealing) yuz beradi.

$1/\sqrt{t}$ Decay Schedule (Kingma & Ba, 2015): Adam konvergensiya teoremasi (Theorem 4.1) va MNIST logistik regressiya tajribalarida qadam kattaligi vaqt qadami $t$ bo'yicha quyidagicha kamaytiriladi:
$$\alpha_t = \frac{\alpha}{\sqrt{t}}$$

Fixed, Drop-Step va Cosine / Cyclical Schedules (Loshchilov & Hutter, 2019): Loshchilov va Hutter (2019, Section 4.1) Adam va AdamW ni uch xil o'rganish tezligi jadvali (fixed learning rate, drop-step schedule va cosine/cyclical schedules) ustida baholab, vazn so'nishini ajratish (Decoupled Weight Decay) optimal learning rate $\alpha$ va weight decay $\lambda$ giperparametrlarini bir-biridan ancha mustaqil qilishini isbotlaganlar.
